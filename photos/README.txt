Photos used by index.html, in the order they appear.

  01  the lead photo — shown large in the gallery
  02  the one landscape shot (marked `wide: true` in the config)
  03-05  the rest

To swap or add one: drop the file here, then update the `photos` list
near the top of the script in index.html. A photo shot landscape needs
`wide: true` so the drifting ribbon gives it the right frame.

The gallery tiles without gaps at 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 or 12 photos.
